# Name collisions — do not attach the wrong company

A thin arrival gives only a company name. Before attaching a domain or one-liner, check the page
is *this* company and not a same-named stranger. Short, evocative startup names collide
constantly: a search for one typically returns a research lab in another country, a nonprofit, a
reseller on a near-identical domain, and a company in an unrelated industry — all under the
same name.

Rule: if the only web trace of a name is plainly a different country, product or business
model, cite nothing. The arrival stays thin — no website, no one-liner. A blank field beats a
stranger's domain.
